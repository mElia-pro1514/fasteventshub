Choose a space that fits.

Start venue planning early. These sample steps help organizers prepare a clear request for the campus events team.

Before you request

Choose a preferred date and backup date, estimate attendance, and note any seating, sound, or display needs.

Venue options

Auditoriums suit larger talks and showcases; classrooms work well for workshops; the central lawn is ideal for outdoor gatherings.

Booking contact

Send your society, event title, expected attendance, and preferred setup to the campus events department.

Good events start with good planning.

Use this quick guide to keep events safe, welcoming, and straightforward to run.

Plan ahead

Submit proposals early and wait for venue confirmation before announcing a final location.

Make it welcoming

Share clear event information, consider accessibility needs, and provide a point of contact for attendees.

Leave it ready

Follow venue guidance, keep walkways clear, and leave the space clean after the event.

A few quick answers.

Common questions from students planning or attending campus events.

Can any student propose an event?

Yes. Share your idea through the proposal form. A society or organizer contact helps the events team follow up.

How do I find events by type?

Use the category filters on the Events page to browse academic, cultural, sports, and career events.

Where do I ask a question?

Use the feedback form on the Contact page to send a message to the demo events department.
