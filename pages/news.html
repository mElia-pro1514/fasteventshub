November 10, 2026

Volunteers wanted for the Winter Code Jam

Help welcome teams, support the workshop rooms, or join the student showcase crew. Volunteer sign-up opens this week.

Read the full update

The Computing Society is looking for student volunteers to help make the Winter Code Jam a great experience. Roles include registration desk support, workshop room assistance, and helping teams prepare for the final showcase. Interested students can contact the event organizers through the society directory.

November 08, 2026

Society sign-ups continue through Friday

Still looking for your people? Society tables will be set up in the student commons during lunch this week.

Read the full update

Societies will be available in the student commons each day from 12:00 to 14:00 through Friday. Meet current members, ask about upcoming activities, and find the group that fits your interests. The online directory has contact information for every listed group.

November 04, 2026

Updated venue request guide is available

The Resource Center now has a quick checklist for room requests and event setup planning.

Read the full update

A new venue booking checklist is available in the Resource Center. It covers the information organizers should prepare before submitting a room request, including preferred dates, attendance estimates, and basic setup needs. Please review the guide before planning your next event.
